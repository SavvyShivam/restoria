Common questions

How does pricing work?

There's no subscription. Your first restore is free. After that, you can watch a short optional ad for another free restore (up to 3 a day), or buy a credit pack. You're only ever charged when you tap Save on a result — previewing before/after never costs anything.

I bought credits but my balance didn't update

Purchases are verified against the App Store / Play Store in the background, which can take a few seconds. If your balance still looks wrong after a minute, force-close and reopen the app. If it's still off, email us with your purchase receipt/date and we'll fix it.

Where do my saved photos go?

Saved results are written to your device's photo gallery and to the app's own storage — never to our servers. Reinstalling the app or clearing its storage won't delete photos already saved to your gallery, but will remove them from the in-app Library.

Do I need an account?

No. Restoria signs you in anonymously on first launch — no email, password, or sign-up screen.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple directly at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Play Store purchases, use Google Play's refund process from your Play Store order history. If a restore failed or a save didn't go through, email us and we'll help sort it out.

Is there an iOS app yet?

Restoria for iOS is coming soon on the App Store — this link will go live once the app is approved. Restoria is currently available for Android.
